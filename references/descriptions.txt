ease is a habit tracker that doesn't feel like work.

no streaks that guilt you. no red warnings. no notifications screaming at you. just a calm, minimal space to build the life you actually want — one small step at a time.

TRACK HABITS YOUR WAY
add up to 4 habits for free, or go unlimited with ease+. each habit lives in a category — movement, mental, nutrition, hydration, sleep, social, financial, or spiritual — so you see the full picture of your well-being, not just a checklist.

ELASTIC STREAKS
missed a day? that's okay. ease uses elastic streaks that forgive one gap day, because real life isn't perfect. your progress doesn't reset from one off day.

MORNING INTENTIONS &amp; EVENING JOURNAL
start your day with one word that sets the tone. end it with a quick reflection. no long prompts, no pressure — just a few seconds of mindfulness that compound over time.

ENERGY-AWARE DAYS
feeling low? ease adapts. on low-energy days, it shows only your top 3 habits and softens the tone. on high-energy days, it encourages you to do more. your app meets you where you are.

INSIGHTS THAT ACTUALLY HELP
see your weekly completion rate, best day of the week, habit stability score, and mood patterns — all visualized in a clean, beautiful interface. premium unlocks 30-day analytics, deep health radar, and energy correlation charts.

FOCUS TIMER
need to get things done? the built-in body doubling timer creates a shared focus space. pick 10, 15, 25, or 45 minutes and work alongside others.

DESIGNED FOR YOU
choose your identity — soft health, quiet wellness, main character, or slow living — and ease personalizes everything: suggested habits, journal prompts, and notification tone. seasonal themes change the entire look of the app.

ease+ PREMIUM
unlock unlimited habits, 30-day analytics, habit stability analysis, best day insights, deep health radar, data export (CSV &amp; PDF), smart per-habit reminders, and more. start with a 7-day free trial.

PRIVACY FIRST
all your data stays on your device. we don't collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. no analytics SDKs. no ads. ever.

ease is for anyone who wants to build better habits without burning out. whether you're tracking water intake, building a morning routine, journaling your thoughts, or just trying to move a little more — ease is your calm companion on the journey.
